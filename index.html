About the band

The Regulars are a four-piece covers band from Kent. Having played together for over 10 years, we perform a mix of Britpop, indie rock and well-known classics from artists including Oasis, Foo Fighters, Kings of Leon, The Killers, Sam Fender, Fleetwood Mac, Pulp and Kaiser Chiefs.

What we play

A mix of Britpop, indie rock, rock classics and songs to dance to.

The Regulars play a mix of Britpop, indie rock, rock classics and songs to dance to, covering songs by artists such as Oasis, Foo Fighters, Kings of Leon, The Killers, Sam Fender, Fleetwood Mac, Pulp, Kaiser Chiefs, The Stone Roses and Bryan Adams.

Our set combines known favourites with songs that aren't found in every covers band's setlist, giving people the chance to sing and dance along to tracks they may not get to hear live.

For special events, we can also learn specific songs by agreement at the time of booking.
